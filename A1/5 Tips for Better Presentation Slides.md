# 5 Tips for Better Presentation Slides

> Video: [5 Tips in 5 Minutes - Presentations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YVgS_opYacQ)  
> Channel: UNC Library

## Main Idea

Slides should support the speaker, not replace the speaker. Keep them clear, simple, and easy to understand.

## 1. Keep It Simple

- The audience should focus on you, not the slides.
- Avoid complicated templates and unnecessary decorations.
- A simple background is often enough.

## 2. Use Less Text

- Do not read directly from your slides.
- Use only a few important words.
- Choose a clear font and make the text large.
- Put only one main idea on each slide.

## 3. Use Good Images

- Images should support your message and create interest.
- Avoid generic stock photos.
- Use clear, high-quality images.
- Make sure you have permission to use them.
- Place the main subject away from the center when possible. This can also leave space for text.

## 4. Simplify Data

- Do not put too many charts or numbers on one slide.
- Show only the data needed to support your main point.
- Make labels and numbers easy to read.
- Keep the new chart accurate to the original data.

## 5. Be Consistent

- Use the same font throughout the presentation.
- Choose two or three main colors.
- Use similar layouts for similar types of slides.
- A consistent design makes the presentation look professional.

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Does each slide have one main idea?
- [ ] Can I remove more text?
- [ ] Is the text large and easy to read?
- [ ] Do the images support my message?
- [ ] Are the charts simple and accurate?
- [ ] Are the fonts, colors, and layouts consistent?

## Final Reminder

> Good slides help the audience understand your message and keep their attention on you.
